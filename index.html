Why ship four languages?

Because each tool does what it's best at — and this page proves it:

  • 🐍Python — the calculator above is real CPython 3.12, compiled to WebAssembly by Pyodide and running at native speed. Business logic stays in the language your backend speaks.
  • ⚙️WebAssembly — the Sync-ID forge is 98 bytes of hand-written WAT, compiled offline with WABT. Zero dependencies, near-native speed.
  • 🌙Lua — the codename oracle runs on Fengari, a full Lua 5.4 VM in JavaScript. The same trick that powers game modding, in a landing page.
